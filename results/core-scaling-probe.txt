work-unit scaling probe
=======================
Each row runs the same work on N threads and reports the aggregate rate and
the per-thread rate. If a unit scales, the per-thread rate stays flat.

arithmetic unit calibrated to 1938965 iterations (~2ms single-threaded)

threads     clock agg/s clock /thread   arith agg/s arith /thread
1                   499           499           501           501
2                   999           500           972           486
4                  1996           499          1840           460
8                  3937           492          2872           359
10                 4666           467          2918           292
12                 4719           393          2890           241

A flat per-thread clock rate means the spin primitive is not the limit; a
falling one means part of the measured 'scaling' is clock-read contention.
